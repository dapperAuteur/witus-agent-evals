Transcript, Mammoth Cave National Park, Kentucky. Guided Historic Tour, guide Dana. Mammoth Cave is the longest known cave system in the world — the guide says more than 400 miles are mapped and surveyors add more every year. It formed in Mississippian limestone under a protective sandstone cap; the Green River carved the base levels. People have entered the cave for about 5,000 years; prehistoric miners left cane torches and gypsum-mining marks. In 1812 the cave supplied saltpeter for gunpowder during the war with Britain, worked by enslaved laborers. Stephen Bishop, an enslaved guide in the 1840s, crossed Bottomless Pit and drew a landmark map from memory. Tuberculosis huts from Dr. John Croghan's failed 1842 underground hospital still stand in the passage. The park was authorized in 1926 and established in 1941, and it is both a UNESCO World Heritage Site (1981) and an International Biosphere Reserve. Eyeless cave fish and cave shrimp live in the underground rivers.
