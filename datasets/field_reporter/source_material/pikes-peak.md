Transcript, Manitou Springs depot, Broadmoor Pikes Peak Cog Railway. Conductor commentary during the ascent. The cog railway opened in 1891, built after founder Zalmon Simmons — the mattress magnate — rode a mule to the summit and decided there had to be a better way. It is the highest railway in North America, topping out at the 14,115-foot summit of Pikes Peak. The route climbs about 7,800 feet over 8.9 miles with grades up to 25 percent, which ordinary adhesion trains cannot manage — hence the Abt rack-and-pinion system. The conductor says the line was rebuilt from the rails up between 2018 and 2021 with new Swiss-made Stadler railcars. Katharine Lee Bates rode to the summit in 1893 and was moved to draft "America the Beautiful." Above treeline around 11,500 feet, bristlecone pines give way to alpine tundra; yellow-bellied marmots whistle at the passing cars. The summit visitor center serves doughnuts fried to a high-altitude recipe.
